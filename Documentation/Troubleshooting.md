# Won't turn on

Does the light go green for a bit then go back to red?

If so, it's most likely that the RPi is browning out. Try a power supply that supports USB-C Power Delivery, as the Raspberry Pi 5 consumes much more power than previous Pis.

If not, it's likely one of these:
* Your power adapter is not plugged in
    * Solution: Make sure it's plugged in firmly on both ends
* Your battery is dead.
    * Solution: Make sure the battery is full before you start testing
* Your cable is damaged.
    * Solution: Use another cable
* The RPI is fried
    * Solution: N/A
